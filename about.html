About the Fest

Date: March 15, 2025
Venue: ITM College Campus

ITM Tech Fest 2025 is an intra-college event celebrating technology, AI, and innovation. It brings together passionate students, developers, and researchers to explore the world of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Robotics through interactive sessions and competitions.

The fest includes workshops, coding contests, AI exhibitions, and keynote talks from industry experts.
